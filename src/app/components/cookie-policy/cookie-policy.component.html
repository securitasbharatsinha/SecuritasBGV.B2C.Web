2. What Are Cookies and Similar Technologies?

Cookies are small text files that are stored on your device when you visit a website. They are widely used by website owners to make their websites work, or to work more efficiently, as well as to provide reporting information.

  • Session cookies are erased when you close your browser.
  • Persistent cookies remain on your device for a set period or until you delete them.
  • First-party cookies are set by us.
  • Third-party cookies enable third-party features or functionality to be provided on or through the website (e.g. advertising, interactive content and analytics).

3. How We Use Cookies

We use cookies to improve your user experience, help us understand how people use our website, and support our marketing efforts. Some cookies are necessary for the website to function properly, while others are optional and require your consent. You can manage your preferences at any time.

5. Consent and Legal Basis

Except for strictly necessary cookies, which are automatic because they are essential for the operation of the Platform, we only place cookies on your device with your explicit consent. When you first visit the Platform, you will be shown a cookie consent banner where you can choose to accept all cookies, reject all cookies, or customize your preferences. You can change your consent preferences at any time.

6. How to Manage Your Cookie Preferences

  • Cookie Consent Tool: You can manage your cookie preferences at any time by clicking the "Cookie Settings" link at the bottom of the page. Allow the page to reload for changes to take effect.
  • Browser Settings: Most web browsers allow you to control cookies through their browser settings. You can find instructions for managing cookies in major browsers' help menus.
  • Do Not Track: Some browsers send a "Do Not Track" signal to websites. We do not currently respond to these signals as there is no uniform industry standard for doing so.
