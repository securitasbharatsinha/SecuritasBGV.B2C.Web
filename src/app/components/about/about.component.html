About us

Transforming Background Verification

For over 25 years, Securitas India, in partnership with Walsons, has been a trusted leader in background verification, ensuring safety, trust, and transparency. We operate across 44 geographies with a reach in 180+ countries, successfully delivering background verification solutions to businesses. Now, we bring the same expertise and reliability to individuals, making verification simple and accessible for everyone.

Whether you are hiring domestic help, renting out your property, or verifying a potential match, we ensure a seamless and stress-free experience. Our services include tenant screening, matrimonial verification, self-verification, and support staff verification.

Where It All Began

Since 2007, Securitas India, powered by Walsons, has helped individuals across the country make safe and informed decisions through reliable background verification.

Backed by cutting-edge technology and a commitment to privacy, we have made a difference in millions of lives verifying criminal records, addresses, education backgrounds, and national identities with speed and accuracy. Every background check we perform adds another layer of confidence to your daily decisions.
