MSME and Gig economy

With increased focus on skill development, the economy is observing the rise of entrepreneurs and skilled professionals for on a contractual basis.

Target Industry: Startups; Entrepreneurs; Freelancers

Recommended Checks: Qualifications, past employment, criminal records, address verification, ID checks, ration card, drugs and alcohol and verifying criminal records of the worker along with a regular governmental check.
